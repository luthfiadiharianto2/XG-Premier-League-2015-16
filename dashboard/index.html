Calibration

Ten quantile bins (equal shots per bin). Points on the diagonal mean predicted xG matches the observed goal rate. Shaded band: 95% interval for the main model.

What moves xG?

Logistic-regression coefficients on standardised inputs: change in log-odds of a goal per 1 SD of each feature.

Is the improvement real?

Difference in log loss, with a 95% interval from a match-level bootstrap (whole matches resampled). Negative = the first model is better.

Method in brief

Finishing: non-penalty goals vs npxG

Small samples. One season is a few dozen shots per player. A z-score between −2 and +2 is consistent with plain luck, and finishing over- or under-performance is known to regress strongly towards zero the next season. Treat this as a scouting prompt, not a verdict.
